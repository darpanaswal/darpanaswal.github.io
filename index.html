Research

LLMSymGuard: A Symbolic Safety Guardrail Framework Leveraging Interpretable Jailbreak Features

Darpan Aswal, Céline Hudelot.

We introduce LLMSymGuard, a symbolic safety guardrail framework for LLMs that leverages Sparse Autoencoder extracted interpretable jailbreak features. We model jailbreak concepts with symbolic logic and structured feature analysis, enabling the design of interpretable safety guardrails.

"Haet Bhasha aur Diskrimineshun": Phonetic Perturbations in Code-Mixed Hinglish to Red-Team LLMs

Darpan Aswal, Siddharth D Jaiswal.

We show that large language models can be tricked into producing unsafe outputs when English and Hindi are mixed in the same sentence and words are misspelled to sound the same. This exposes a tokenization vulnerability that lets harmful prompts slip through guardrails, raising serious concerns about the safety of LLMs in multilingual contexts.

Non-Euclidean Hierarchical Representational Learning using Hyperbolic Graph Neural Networks for Environmental Claim Detection

Darpan Aswal, Manjira Sinha.

We study GNNs for detecting environmental claims, offering an efficient and interpretable alternative to LLMs. We show that GNNs and Hyperbolic GNNs can match or surpass transformer accuracy while using 30× fewer parameters, enabling lightweight and transparent claim verification.
