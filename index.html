Hello! I am Darpan Aswal, a first year Master's in Computer Science (Informatique - AI Track) student at Université Paris-Saclay. My research interests lie in the intersection of AI Safety, Interpretability and Reasoning!

Currently, I am working at the MICS Laboratory, CentraleSupélec with Professor Céline Hudelot to develop concept extraction frameworks for interpreting the reasoning mechanisms in Large Diffusion Language Models. Prior to that, I worked on designing a logical safety guardrail mechanism for LLMs--a first of it's kind--by utilizing Sparse Autoencoder learned adversarial features.

I obtained my bachelor's degree in Electronics and Electrical Communication Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur, where I worked in the CNeRG Laboratory, IIT Kharagpur with Professor Animesh Mukherjee on Red Teaming Large Language Models. I pursued my bachelor's thesis with in Environmental Claim Detection using Hyperbolic Graph Neural Nets.
